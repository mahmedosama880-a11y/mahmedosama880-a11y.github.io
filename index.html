Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard built with Power Query to transform and analyze the Superstore retail dataset. I ran a complete ETL process, cleaning and transforming 9,994 records, then built KPIs and visualizations to support data-driven decisions.

It covers Total Sales, Total Profit and Total Quantity Sold, regional and customer-segment performance, the top 10 states by sales, and monthly trends from 2016 to 2019. Slicers for Year, Region and Category let users explore the data.

Key insights: the West region had the highest sales and profitability, and the Consumer segment contributed the most to sales and profit. California ranked first by sales, followed by New York and Texas.

Case study: Superstore Sales & Performance

How the dashboard answers real business questions. Values below are taken directly from the dashboard.

Business problem

Which products, customers and regions drive sales and profit, and where is performance falling behind?

Data preparation

Cleaning and structuring the sales data in Power BI so it is ready for modeling and reporting.

Analysis

DAX measures and visuals that compare sales, profit and operations across products, customers and regions.

Insights from the dashboard

  • Sales grew from about $0.48M in 2016 to about $0.73M in 2019, with a small dip in 2017.
  • Phones and Chairs are the top sub-categories by sales (about $0.33M each), followed by Storage.
  • Tables is the clearest loss-maker: sales of about $0.21M but negative profit.
  • Around 81% of order records are profitable.
  • California is the largest state by sales ($457,688).
